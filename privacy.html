PART I — Understanding Toju

About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Toju Health ("Toju," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information through the Toju Health platform and related services.

Toju is a health information coordination platform designed to help individuals organise and coordinate health information for themselves and, where properly authorised, for family members. Toju is a coordination and information-management service. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, interpret medical results, recommend treatment, or replace the judgment of a qualified healthcare professional.

Because the information handled through Toju may include sensitive health information, we apply additional safeguards to its collection, use, access, and disclosure.

Purpose of This Policy

This Privacy Policy is intended to help you understand:

  1. what personal information we collect and how we collect it;
  2. how we use and protect personal and health information;
  3. how documents and other health information may be processed using optical character recognition (OCR) and artificial intelligence (AI);
  4. when and why information may be shared with service providers and other parties;
  5. how information may be stored or processed across jurisdictions;
  6. how long we retain information and how it may be deleted;
  7. the rights and choices available to you; and
  8. how you can contact us or raise a privacy concern.

This Policy should be read together with any other notices or terms we provide when you use particular Toju features or services.

Who This Policy Applies To

This Policy applies to individuals who use Toju as:

  1. Profile Owners: individuals whose health information is maintained in a Toju profile. A Profile Owner may use Toju for their own information. Where another person sets up a profile for them, the Profile Owner is asked to agree by an emailed link, and can withdraw that agreement at any time without needing a Toju account.
  2. Coordinators: individuals who create a Toju account and use it to help manage another person's health information, after that person has been invited to agree.
  3. Website visitors and waitlist members: individuals who visit toju.health or give us their email address to hear about the beta or launch.

Note:

  • An individual may be both a Profile Owner and a Coordinator.
  • Each profile is currently managed from a single Toju account. Sharing one profile between several accounts is not yet available.
  • Being a family member, caregiver, or other person connected to a Profile Owner does not, by itself, give that person access to the Profile Owner's information. Agreement is required before a Coordinator can manage another person's health information through Toju.
  • Where a Coordinator uploads information relating to another person, the fact that the Coordinator uploaded the information does not, by itself, make the Coordinator the Profile Owner or give the Coordinator unrestricted rights over that information.

Services Covered by This Policy

  • This Policy applies to personal information processed through Toju's websites, applications, platform features, and related services that link to or expressly adopt this Policy.
  • Toju may also provide support, communications, account-management functions, and other services connected with the platform. This Policy applies to the personal information processed through those services to the extent described here.
  • Third-party websites, applications, or services that are not operated by Toju may have their own privacy policies. Where Toju uses a third party to process information on its behalf, that processing is addressed under the sections of this Policy concerning service providers and information sharing.

Our Approach to Privacy

Toju is designed around the principle that individuals should have meaningful control over their health information.

Where Toju processes information about a Profile Owner, the Profile Owner remains the person whose information is being managed, even where another person coordinates that information on their behalf. Toju's approach is therefore based on the Profile Owner's agreement rather than family relationship alone.

Subject to the applicable law and the functionality of the service:

  • individuals can manage information relating to themselves;
  • a Profile Owner is asked to agree before a Coordinator manages their health information, and can withdraw that agreement at any time;
  • when a Profile Owner withdraws their agreement, we delete their health records and health details, as described under Retention & Deletion;
  • we seek to limit the collection, use, disclosure, and retention of information to what is reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy;
  • AI-assisted processing is subject to human review before information extracted from documents is added to a health timeline; and
  • we do not sell personal health information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Applicable Privacy Requirements

Toju Health is based in Canada and is designed to support individuals in Canada who may coordinate health information relating to family members in Nigeria and other jurisdictions. Our privacy practices are intended to address applicable privacy and data protection requirements, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) in Canada and the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA) where applicable. AI-Assisted Processing

Toju uses automated technologies, including artificial intelligence, to assist with the organisation of information contained in uploaded health documents. These technologies are used to support information management and are not intended to provide medical advice or make healthcare decisions.

How the Process Works

When you upload a photograph, PDF, or other health document:

  1. Document storage: The uploaded document is stored in a private storage area within Toju's systems so that it can be processed and made available to the account that uploaded it.
  2. Text extraction: The document is read by Tesseract, an open-source OCR tool that runs inside Toju's own systems, to identify and extract the text it contains. The document is not sent to another company for this step.
  3. Information structuring: Where Toju's own reading of a document needs help, the extracted text, not the original image, may be sent to our AI provider, OpenAI, which organises it into structured fields, such as medication, diagnosis, date, or healthcare provider. The text may include personal details that appear in the document, such as names, dates, and provider details.
  4. Human review: The resulting information is presented for user review. It must be confirmed or corrected before it is added to the user's health timeline as structured information.

The providers used for these functions are named under Service Providers, and may change. We will update this Policy before we start using a different provider for these functions.

What AI Does Not Do

Toju's AI-assisted features are intended to organise information that already appears in a user's documents. They are not designed to:

  1. diagnose a medical condition;
  2. determine whether a diagnosis is correct;
  3. recommend or prescribe treatment;
  4. determine whether medication should be started, stopped, or changed;
  5. provide medical advice; or
  6. make healthcare decisions on behalf of a user.

Accuracy and Human Review

Automated systems can misread, omit, or incorrectly structure information. For that reason, information generated through automated processing is not treated as confirmed medical information solely because an AI system produced it.

Users should review extracted information against the original document and correct any errors before relying on it for coordination purposes. Toju does not represent that automated extraction will always be complete or accurate.

Use of Third-Party AI and Processing Services

Toju uses third-party technology providers to perform AI-assisted structuring, hosting, storage, security, and related functions. These providers process information on Toju's behalf and are subject to contractual, technical, and organisational safeguards appropriate to the services they provide.

No Training Use of User Health Information

We do not use users' health information to train AI models. Under our agreement with OpenAI, the text we send is not used to train its models. OpenAI may keep that text for a limited period for security and abuse monitoring, and our healthcare agreement with OpenAI restricts how it may use or share it. We send text only, never the original document image.

We will update this Policy if our AI processing arrangements change in a way that affects these statements.

AI Processing Does Not Change Your Rights

The use of automated processing does not remove or reduce any privacy rights you have under applicable law. You may contact Toju regarding questions about how your information is processed, including through automated technologies, subject to applicable legal requirements and the limitations of the service.

PART IV — How We Protect and Manage It

Retention & Deletion

Toju retains personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, to provide and maintain the service, and to meet applicable legal, regulatory, security, and recordkeeping requirements.

How Long We Keep Information

Health documents and structured health information are kept for as long as the relevant profile or account remains active, unless the information is deleted earlier or a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Toju will periodically assess whether personal information continues to be necessary. Information that is no longer required will be securely deleted, anonymised, or otherwise disposed of in accordance with our retention practices and applicable law.

Deleting Your Information

Deleting records and documents. A Coordinator can delete individual records and uploaded documents in the app, where the app offers that option.

When a Profile Owner withdraws their agreement. We delete the health records in the profile, clear the health details held in it (date of birth, gender, blood group, blood pressure, heart rate, weight, height, allergies, conditions, and location), and delete the uploaded documents. This happens straight away. We keep only a minimal record: the name on the profile, the name and email address of the person invited, the dates of the invitation, response and withdrawal, the consent notice version, the IP address used, and an audit log entry. We keep this record for 2 years so that we can show that agreement was given and withdrawn, and then delete it, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Other requests. To have your account, profiles, or waitlist email deleted, email abolaji@toju.health. We will complete the deletion within 30 days, except where we must keep information as described below. Closing an account is done by emailing us.

Backups. Copies held in backups are overwritten on our provider's normal backup cycle, within 30 days.

If a Profile Owner has died or cannot manage their own information. A legally authorised representative, such as an executor or substitute decision-maker, may contact us with proof of their authority to withdraw agreement and request deletion. We will not give anyone access to a profile beyond the access the Coordinator already has.

We may also keep information where required or permitted by law, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, investigate security incidents, or prevent fraud. Where information is kept for one of these purposes, we will limit its use to that purpose.

Security

Toju takes reasonable technical, administrative, and organisational measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, collection, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, destruction, or other misuse.

The safeguards applied include:

  1. sign-in authentication;
  2. access rules in the database, so each account can reach only the profiles and records it owns;
  3. private file storage, with documents readable only by the account that uploaded them;
  4. encryption of information in transit, and encryption of stored data at rest by our hosting provider;
  5. an audit log of consent events;
  6. safeguards governing access by service providers;
  7. procedures for managing security vulnerabilities and incidents; and
  8. confidentiality and security requirements for anyone who works with Toju.

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure. Accordingly, while Toju works to protect information using reasonable safeguards, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Users are also responsible for protecting their account credentials and withdrawal links, using appropriate device security, and notifying Toju promptly if they believe their account or information has been accessed without authorisation.

Toju maintains procedures for identifying, assessing, containing, investigating, documenting, and responding to suspected or confirmed privacy and security incidents.

Where an incident occurs, Toju will assess:

  1. what information was involved;
  2. whose information may have been affected;
  3. the nature and extent of the incident;
  4. the likelihood and severity of potential harm;
  5. whether the information was accessed, used, disclosed, lost, or otherwise compromised; and

We keep a record of every incident for at least 24 months.

Toju may also take other reasonable measures to reduce the risk of harm, including securing affected systems, restricting access, resetting credentials, investigating the cause, and implementing measures to prevent recurrence.

Rights Under the NDPA

Where the NDPA applies, individuals have the rights provided under the Act, subject to its statutory conditions, exceptions, and limitations. These include rights to:

  • receive information about the processing of their personal data;
  • request access to their personal data and relevant information about its processing;
  • request correction or completion of inaccurate or incomplete personal data;
  • request erasure where the statutory requirements for erasure are satisfied;
  • request restriction of processing in circumstances provided by law;
  • object to certain processing;
  • withdraw consent where consent is the applicable legal basis for processing;
  • receive or request transfer of personal data where the applicable portability requirements are satisfied; and
  • exercise applicable rights concerning decisions based solely on automated processing, including profiling.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing lawfully carried out before withdrawal. It also does not require Toju to stop processing where another lawful basis permits or requires continued processing.
