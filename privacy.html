PART I — Understanding Toju

About this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Toju Health ("Toju," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, discloses, and otherwise processes personal information through the Toju Health platform and related services.

Toju is a health information coordination platform designed to help individuals organise and coordinate health information for themselves and, where properly authorised, for family members. Toju is a coordination and information-management service. It does not provide medical advice, diagnose conditions, interpret medical results, recommend treatment, or replace the judgment of a qualified healthcare professional.

Because the information handled through Toju may include sensitive health information, we apply additional safeguards to its collection, use, access, and disclosure.

Purpose of This Policy

This Privacy Policy is intended to help you understand:

  • what personal information we collect and how we collect it;
  • how we use and protect personal and health information;
  • how documents are read, and how optional AI help works;
  • when and why information may be shared with people you choose, service providers and other parties;
  • where information is stored and processed;
  • how long we retain information and how it may be deleted;
  • the rights and choices available to you; and
  • how you can contact us or raise a privacy concern.

This Policy should be read together with our Terms of Use and any other notices we provide when you use particular Toju features.

Who This Policy Applies To

This Policy applies to individuals who use Toju as:

  • Profile Owners: individuals whose health information is kept in a Toju profile. A Profile Owner has their own Toju account and controls their profile, including who else can see or add to it.
  • Coordinators: individuals given access by a Profile Owner to help manage that Profile Owner's health information, for example an adult child helping a parent.
  • Viewers: individuals given view-only access by a Profile Owner, who can see and download that Profile Owner's records but not change them.
  • Website visitors and waitlist members: individuals who visit toju.health or give us their email address to hear about the beta or launch.

Note:

  • An individual may be both a Profile Owner and a Coordinator.
  • A Profile Owner can give access to more than one Coordinator, and can remove any of them at any time.
  • Being a family member, caregiver, or other person connected to a Profile Owner does not, by itself, give that person access to the Profile Owner's information. The Profile Owner's agreement is required.
  • Where a Coordinator adds information to a Profile Owner's profile, that information belongs to the Profile Owner's health record. Adding it does not make the Coordinator the Profile Owner or give the Coordinator unrestricted rights over it.

Services Covered by This Policy

  • This Policy applies to personal information processed through Toju's websites, applications, platform features, and related services that link to or expressly adopt this Policy.
  • Toju may also provide support, communications, account-management functions, and other services connected with the platform. This Policy applies to the personal information processed through those services to the extent described here.
  • Third-party websites, applications, or services that are not operated by Toju may have their own privacy policies. Where Toju uses a third party to process information on its behalf, that processing is addressed under Service Providers.

Our Approach to Privacy

Toju is designed around the principle that individuals should have meaningful control over their health information. The Profile Owner remains the person whose information is being managed, even where someone else helps coordinate it. Toju's approach is therefore based on the Profile Owner's agreement, not on family relationship alone.

Subject to applicable law and the functionality of the service:

AI-Assisted Processing

Toju reads uploaded documents itself. AI help is an option you choose for each document. It helps organise information that is already in the document; it does not give medical advice or make healthcare decisions.

How it works. When you upload a photograph, PDF, or Word document:

  • Checks and storage: Toju first checks the file's real type from its contents, and refuses files that carry scripts, macros or embedded content, or are protected by a password. Photographs are re-saved without location, camera and time details. The document is then stored in a private storage area in Canada, readable only by the Profile Owner and the people they have given access.
  • Reading the document: Toju reads the text on its own servers, using Tesseract, an open-source tool. The document is not sent to another company for this step.
  • Optional AI help: on the upload screen, a "Let AI help read this document" switch is off by default every time. If you switch it on, and Toju could not find the document's title, date, or healthcare provider by itself, Toju sends the document's text to our AI provider, OpenAI, in the United States, to help find those details. Before it is sent, Toju removes the patient's name, date of birth, email addresses, phone numbers, and identification numbers. The image or file itself is never sent. Each use is recorded in the activity history.
  • Human review: the details found are shown to you, and must be confirmed or corrected before they are added to the health timeline.

Removing those identifiers lowers the risk but does not make the text anonymous. Other details in the document, such as clinic names, test names, and results, are still sent. If you would rather nothing leave Toju's servers, leave AI help switched off.

What AI does not do. Toju's AI help is not designed to diagnose a condition; decide whether a diagnosis is correct; recommend or prescribe treatment; decide whether medication should be started, stopped, or changed; provide medical advice; or make healthcare decisions for anyone.

Accuracy. Automated reading can misread, miss, or misplace information. Details produced this way are not treated as confirmed medical information. Check them against the original document before relying on them. Toju does not promise that automated reading is always complete or accurate.

No training on your health information. We do not use health information to train AI models. Under our agreement with OpenAI, the text we send is not used to train its models. OpenAI may keep that text for a limited period for security and abuse monitoring, and our healthcare agreement with OpenAI restricts how it may use or share it.

We will update this Policy before our AI processing changes in a way that affects these statements. Using automated processing does not reduce any privacy rights you have under applicable law.

Emergency Card

The Emergency Card is a one-page summary of a profile's key health and contact information, for a doctor, neighbour or relative to read in an emergency.

Who can create it. The Profile Owner and Coordinators can fill in the card. Sharing it by link is for the Profile Owner, and for Coordinators only if the Profile Owner switches that on. Viewers can download or print the card but cannot create links. The card shows only the emergency details entered for that profile: for example conditions, current medicines, allergies, blood group, doctor and pharmacy contacts, and emergency contacts.

How it is shared. The card can be downloaded as a PDF or shared through a secure link. A link lasts 24 hours unless the person creating it chooses 1 hour or 7 days instead, and the Profile Owner, or whoever created the link, can turn it off at any time; it then stops working immediately. Anyone with an active link can view the card without a Toju account. The link uses a long random code, is not shown to search engines, and shows only the card, never records or documents. Creating a card, creating or turning off a link, and each time a link is opened are recorded in the activity history.

Copies outside Toju. Once a card is downloaded, printed or forwarded, for example by WhatsApp or email, Toju cannot control or delete those copies. The privacy practices of the app used to send it, and of the person who receives it, then apply.

Other people's details. If you add an emergency contact, only include them with their permission. Their name, relationship and phone number are used only to show on the profile and the card.

Not an emergency service. Toju does not monitor the card or contact anyone in an emergency. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

PART IV — How We Protect and Manage It

Retention & Deletion

Toju keeps personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes it was collected for, to provide the service, and to meet legal, security, and record-keeping requirements.

How long we keep information. Health records and documents are kept while the Profile Owner's account is active, unless deleted earlier. If no one has signed in to an account for 12 months, we will email the account holder. If they do not reply, we will delete the account and its information within 6 months after that. Waitlist email addresses are kept until you join the beta, unsubscribe, or ask us to delete them.

Deleting records and documents. The Profile Owner, and Coordinators with permission, can delete individual records and documents in the app.

Erasing health data. A Profile Owner can erase all health records and documents in their profile while keeping their account.

Emergency Card. A card's link stops working when it expires (after 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days, as chosen), or when it is turned off. The card itself is kept while the profile exists and is deleted with the profile, or earlier if the Profile Owner deletes it. Copies that were downloaded or forwarded are outside Toju's control.

Deleting an account. When an account is deleted, we remove:

  • the login and the profile;
  • all of the person's health records and documents, including uploads they had not finished saving;
  • access they gave to others, and access they had to other people's profiles; and
  • their consent records and access-request history.

We keep two things, unlinked from the deleted person:

  • records the person added to someone else's profile, because they form part of that other person's health record; and
  • the activity history of other people's profiles, so there is still a record of what was done to those Profile Owners' records. The history records only that a change was made, never what a field said.

Other requests. To have your account, profiles, or waitlist email deleted, or for help with any of the above, email abolaji@toju.health. We will complete the deletion within 30 days, except where we must keep information as described below.

Backups. Our database is backed up daily, and each backup is kept for 7 days. Deleted information therefore disappears from backups within 7 days.

If a Profile Owner has died or cannot manage their own information. A legally authorised representative, such as an executor or substitute decision-maker, may contact us with proof of their authority to remove access or request deletion. We will not give anyone access to a profile beyond the access the Profile Owner already gave.

We may also keep information where required or permitted by law, or where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims, investigate security incidents, or prevent fraud. Where we keep information for one of these purposes, we limit its use to that purpose.

Security

Toju takes reasonable technical, administrative, and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, use, disclosure, alteration, loss, or destruction.

Our safeguards include:

  • hosting on Supabase, which holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 certification;
  • sign-in with a minimum password length, checks that reject passwords known to have leaked, and limits on repeated sign-in attempts;
  • access rules in the database, so each person can reach only the profiles they own or have been given access to, with automated tests that check one family cannot see another's records;
  • private file storage, with limits on file size; each upload's real file type is checked before it is stored, and files carrying scripts, macros or embedded content are refused; photographs are re-saved without location, camera and time details; document links that expire after 60 seconds;
  • encryption of information in transit, and encryption of stored information by our hosting provider;
  • an activity history of actions on each profile;
  • data-processing agreements with our service providers; and
  • procedures for managing security issues and incidents, and confidentiality and data-handling requirements for everyone who works with Toju.

No method of transmitting or storing information is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. You can help by protecting your password, keeping your devices secure, and telling us promptly if you think your account or information has been accessed without permission.

Privacy incidents. A privacy incident is any unauthorised access to, use, disclosure, loss, alteration, or destruction of personal information. Toju has a procedure for identifying, assessing, containing, investigating, recording, and responding to incidents. We assess what information was involved, whose, how, the likelihood and severity of harm, and what is needed to contain and fix it.

Where an incident creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify the affected individuals and the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, as PIPEDA requires. Where the NDPA applies and requires it, we will notify the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of the incident, and tell affected individuals without delay where the risk is high. We keep a record of every incident for at least 24 months.

Nigeria-Specific Information

Applicability. Toju may process personal data about people in Nigeria, including where the Profile Owner lives in Nigeria and a Coordinator lives elsewhere. Where the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA) applies, Toju will comply with the requirements that apply to that processing. We have written to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC) about registration, and will register if required. Health information is sensitive personal data under the NDPA and receives the additional protection that applies to it.

Your rights. Where the NDPA applies, you have the rights it provides, subject to its conditions and exceptions, including rights to be informed about processing; access your data; correct or complete it; erase it where the conditions are met; restrict or object to processing; withdraw consent; receive or transfer your data where portability applies; and rights concerning decisions based solely on automated processing. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing carried out lawfully before withdrawal.

Cross-border processing. Toju is based in Canada and stores information in Canada, with only optional AI help processed in the United States, as described under Where Your Information Is Stored and Processed. Personal data about people in Nigeria is therefore transferred outside Nigeria. Where the NDPA applies, Toju will meet its requirements for cross-border transfers, which may include the individual's consent, contractual safeguards, or another mechanism permitted by Nigerian law.

Exercising them. Send requests using the contact details below. If you are not satisfied, you may lodge a complaint with the NDPC or pursue another remedy under Nigerian law.

Children and Other Vulnerable Persons

Toju is designed for adults. We do not knowingly create accounts for children without an appropriate legal basis and, where required, the authorisation of a parent or legal guardian.

Anyone who helps manage another person's information must have the authority or permission required. Family relationship, caregiving status, or having another person's health records does not, by itself, give unrestricted access to that person's Toju profile. If an adult cannot give agreement for themselves, for example because of illness or loss of capacity, their legally authorised representative may do so on their behalf, and we may ask for evidence of that authority.

If we learn that information was collected or accessed without the required authorisation, we will investigate and address it as the law requires.

Third-Party Services

Toju uses the service providers described under Service Providers. Toju may also link to services run independently of Toju; those are governed by their own privacy policies. Where you choose to give information directly to an independent third party, that party's privacy practices apply once it is outside Toju's control.
