How Toju works

From scattered to structured, in four steps

1

Upload what you already have

Photos of prescriptions, lab results, doctor's notes, even the WhatsApp voice note your sister sent — Toju accepts it as-is. No re-typing, no reformatting.

2

AI reads and extracts the details

Toju identifies the medication, the date, the provider, the result — and structures it, so nobody has to squint at a blurry photo to find what matters.

3

You confirm what it found

Every extraction is shown to a family member for a quick confirm-or-correct — Toju never adds anything to the record without a human checking it first.

4

The whole family sees one timeline

Every rep with access — you, your siblings, whoever's involved — sees the same chronological history. Searchable. Shareable with a doctor in one link.

Security & trust

This is your family's health information. We treat it that way.

In our early conversations with diaspora families, the same concerns came up every time: who can see this, is it encrypted, and will it be used to train some AI model somewhere. Here's where we stand on each.

  • Coordination, not diagnosis. Toju organizes and structures information. It never interprets, suggests, or replaces a doctor's judgment.
  • Access is permission-based. A profile is only visible to the family members explicitly invited to it — nobody else, ever.
  • Built toward PIPEDA and HIPAA-aligned standards. Our data architecture is being built around Canadian and U.S. health-privacy frameworks from day one, not bolted on after.
  • No ads. No data sold. Toju is funded by subscriptions, not by monetizing your family's health data.

Questions

Before you ask

Is Toju a medical diagnosis tool?

No. Toju organizes and structures the information your family already has — documents, results, notes. It never interprets results or suggests treatment. That stays with your doctors.

Who can see my parent's information?

Only the family members explicitly invited to that profile. Nobody else — not other Toju users, not the public, not advertisers.

Will my family's data be used to train an AI model?

No. We use enterprise AI services that don't train their models on customer data by default, and we're building our pipeline specifically to keep raw documents out of reach of any model that isn't doing the extraction task you asked for.

What if my parent doesn't use email or isn't tech-savvy?

Toju is built for the family member coordinating from abroad first. Your parent doesn't need to do anything — you and your siblings manage the profile on their behalf.

Which countries does Toju work for?

Toju is built for African diaspora families broadly — wherever you are, and wherever your parent's care is happening.

How do I get access?

Join the beta waitlist above. We're onboarding families in small groups so we can get feedback right as you use it.
